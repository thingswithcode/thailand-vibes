Destinations / Bangkok

Where to eat

Food & drinks in Bangkok

Bangkok has so much to offer on the cuisine side, and here are my favorite spots.

Where to eat

01

Thai food

The Local

Extremely high quality, delicious Thai dishes and a very traditional decor. They serve unlimited rice with all dishes. On the pricier end, but if you’re in for a treat, that’s your place.

Price
10 to 14 € per dish

02

Thai food

Shamballa Somtam

Conveniently located in one of my favourite neighborhoods, Ari, with all of the delicious Thai food. Not street food cheap, but almost.

Price
about 4 € per dish

03

Thai food

Taling Pling Sukhumvit 34

They have several branches, but this one has a very cute garden with a little pond, and any Thai dish you could wish for.

Price
6 to 7 € per dish
Open in Google Maps ↗
04

Thai food

NAAM 1608

Right at the Chao Phraya river, with very nice outdoor seating looking over the water, seeing the boats go by while you have lunch or dinner. The food is really good too.

Price
8 to 9 € per dish
Open in Google Maps ↗
05

Thai food

Nhong Rim Klong

More of a canteen style, plastic chair kind of vibe, but the food definitely makes up for the lack of chic ambience. They’re specialized in seafood and it’s sooo good!

Price
8 to 10 € per dish
Open in Google Maps ↗
06

Thai food

Baan Suan Sathon

The opposite: a super cute restaurant and cafe where you sit in absolute greenery, with an artificial waterfall and a pond with koi. The food is good but not extraordinary, the place is super cute though.

Price
8 to 9 € per dish
Open in Google Maps ↗
07

Thai food

Supanniga Eating Room, Tha Tian

A fabulous restaurant with a beautiful view right on Wat Arun, especially beautiful after sunset. It’s basically always fully booked, so make sure to reserve online before you go.

Price
8 to 18 € per dish
Open in Google Maps ↗
08

Thai food

Mae Varee

For mango sticky rice. That’s the only thing they sell, and people are usually queuing up in front.

Price
2.50 to 4 €

What we recommend everywhere

The things that work in every corner of the country, from the street food stall to the temple door.

All over Thailand

Food

Pad Krapao Moo with a kai dao

Minced pork stir-fried with chili, garlic and Thai basil, served with rice. It’s very spicy, so order medium or not spicy, and get a ‘kai dao’ (fried egg) on top. You can have it with chicken, beef or shrimp too.

Food

Mango sticky rice

For me, the best dessert, I cannot get enough of it. Make sure to try it if you like mango.

Drinks

Singha over Chang

Grab a Singha, I think it’s so much better than Chang, and watch as people go by.

Temple

Cover shoulders and knees

At temples, cover your shoulders and knees and take your shoes off when you see the sign (don’t worry, nobody will steal them). Taking photos is totally fine, just be mindful of locals praying.

Wellness

Who goes to Thailand and does not get a massage?

A 60 min aroma massage at a nice spa is about 1,300 Baht, regular salons charge 10 to 20 dollars an hour. I’d go for an oil or a foot massage and check the Google Maps reviews first.

Market

Tell the vendors if you don't like spicy

And as a general rule, I would avoid eating raw fish at markets.

Shopping

Bring back dried mango

Brands like H&M or Zara are more expensive here than in Europe. Fill your suitcase with dried mango and souvenirs instead, everyone back home loves it.

Food

Gai Pad Med Mamuang

Chicken fried with vegetables and cashew nuts, Mike’s favourite. It’s not very spicy, so if you don’t really eat spicy, this one is for you.

Food

Pad See Ew

If you like noodles: stir-fried with soy sauce only, so it’s not spicy, and you can have it with any protein.

Drinks

Fruit shakes without sugar

Fruit shakes are sold everywhere and they’re extremely good. Try pineapple, coconut and banana. They add sugar syrup by default, so say ‘mai ao namdtan’ if you want it without.

Food

Coconut ice cream from the cart

Look out for the guys with little ice cream carts, they ring a bell to let you know they’re coming.

Food

Vegan or vegetarian?

No problem: most dishes come with a tofu option, and it’s fairly easy in Thailand to get food without meat.

Getting around

The Grand Palace is basically never closed

If a TukTuk driver tells you it is, he wants to take you to five jewelry stores. Don’t let him.

Market

Bargain at the markets

There usually won’t be prices displayed, which means: bargain your heart out.

Bangkok

This is the place we spent most of our time since we moved here. So there's lots of recommendations, from temples, markets and rooftops to nightlife, plus things we wouldn't recommend.

01
Temple

Wat Paknam Phasi Charoen

You honestly can't leave Thailand without visiting at least one temple, so here's my favorite. Less touristy than the Grand Palace, absolutely stunning, and nobody bothers you. Step inside the white stupa and climb its five levels to the emerald statues on the top floor.

Open
Daily 6am to 8pm
Entry
Free
Wear
Shoulders and knees covered, shoes off at the sign
Another nice templeWat Arun, where you can get dressed in a traditional Thai outfit and get your photos taken.

02
Market

Jodd Fairs

Markets are one of my favorite things about Thailand. Jodd Fairs is not too big and not too small, with food, drinks, clothing and jewelry. It's 'farang' friendly, there's usually live music, so grab a Singha and watch as people go by.

Open
Daily 5pm to 12am
Try
Mango sticky rice, Pad Krapao Moo with a kai dao
More marketsChatuchak, the huge weekend market (Sat and Sun 6am to 6pm), and the Train Night Market DanNeramit with its Disney-like castle (Thu to Sun 5pm to 12am).
Open in Google Maps ↗
03
Rooftop

Tichuca

Forget the rooftop from The Hangover and visit this gem instead. Great vibes, good music, right in the centre of Sukhumvit. Go for sunset. Cocktails are a little pricey for Thailand, but the view is totally worth it.

Open
Sun to Thu 5pm to 12am, Fri and Sat until 1:30am
After
Club 'Chupa' in the same building
More rooftopsOctave right across, with a live DJ. Vertigo at Banyan Tree on the 61st floor. Pastel for party vibes, free shots and dancing until 1am.
I would skipSky Bar at Lebua: the view is not that amazing, it's pretty dead and the drinks are outrageously expensive.

04
Getting around no fixed spot

Hop on a TukTuk

Kind of obvious, but too fun to miss. Take one after sunset, from the rooftop to dinner or Khao San Road. Bargain a little. Enjoy driving through skyscrapers at crazy speed with blasting loud speakers.

05
Food

Chinatown after sunset

One of the largest Chinatowns in the world. The bustling vibes with tons of really great street food are incomparable. Search for Yaowarat Road.

Open
All the time, best after sunset

06
Shopping

IconSiam

Probably my favorite mall. Take the boat from Oriental Pier or Sathon Pier, it makes the visit even more special. Inside there's a waterfall, a small floating market, a Starbucks with river views and a fountain show at 7, 8 and 9pm.

Open
Daily 10am to 10pm
Tip
Fill your suitcase with dried mango, not overpriced brands
Other mallsPlatinum Mall for bargains, MBK for fakes, Central World, Emquartier and Emsphere with the TRIBE rooftop beach club, Siam Paragon and then Siam Square.
Open in Google Maps ↗
07
Wellness several locations

Get a massage

Who goes to Thailand and does not get a massage? My two favorites are Infinity Spa and Divana. A 60 min aroma massage at Infinity is about 1,300 Baht, with refreshments before and mango sticky rice after. Regular salons are 10 to 20 dollars an hour.

Infinity
Daily 9:30am to 9:30pm
Divana
Daily 10am to 11pm
My tipI personally don't enjoy Thai massage, it feels like stretching for lazy people. I usually go for a full body oil massage or a foot massage, and check the Google Maps reviews.
08
Food all over the city

Try local (street) food

When I first came here I was so worried about food poisoning that I basically had fried rice all day. Don't make that mistake. Try Gai Pad Med Mamuang (Mike's favourite, not spicy), Pad Krapao Moo, fried seabass in a salt crust and Pad See Ew.

My favourite Thai restaurantsThe Local, Shamballa Somtam in Ari, Taling Pling Sukhumvit 34, NAAM 1608 on the river, Nhong Rim Klong for seafood, Supanniga Eating Room with a view on Wat Arun, Mae Varee for mango sticky rice.
09
Nightlife

Party at Khao San Road

The party street for backpackers, definitely an experience. Blasting music from every bar, roasted scorpions, and buckets full of cocktails at every corner. They're strong as hell, so I'd switch to beer after the first one.

Bars
The ONE at Khao San, then 'the Club'. For chill vibes: The Macaroni Club

10
Nightlife

Soi Cowboy

Sukhumvit between Nana and Phrom Phong after sunset is a whole different vibe. People watching and strolling through Soi Cowboy, also known from The Hangover, is extremely interesting. I wouldn't spend too much time though.

Open in Google Maps ↗
10+1
Nature

Recharge on Bang Kachao

A green island right in the heart of Bangkok. Rent a bike, ride through the lanes, stroll by the water, have lunch and very good coffee. One of my favorite places to get away from the hustle and bustle.

Getting there
Taxi to Wat Khlong Toei Nok Pier, boat across (ask for the small 20 Baht boat)
Eat
Hidden Woods, Coconut Lane

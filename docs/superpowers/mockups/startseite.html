The capital

Bangkok

The hustle and bustle of the heart of Thailand is absolutely fascinating. Amazing rooftop bars, extremely good restaurants, beautiful temples, the best massages and bustling markets.

All over Thailand

What we recommend everywhere

The things that work in every corner of the country, from the street food stall to the temple door.

AllFood & drinksTemplesMarketsWellnessShopping
Food

Pad Krapao Moo with a kai dao

Spicy minced pork with Thai basil and rice. Order a fried egg on top.

Dessert

Mango sticky rice

For me, the best dessert. Make sure to try it if you like mango.

Drinks

Singha over Chang

I think it's so much better. Grab one and watch as people go by.

Temples

Cover shoulders and knees

Shoes off at the sign. Photos are fine, just be mindful of locals praying.

Wellness

Who goes to Thailand and does not get a massage?

60 min aroma massage at a nice spa: about 1,300 Baht.

Markets

Tell the vendors if you don't like spicy

And as a general rule, I would avoid eating raw fish at markets.

Shopping

Bring back dried mango

Brands cost more here than in Europe. Everyone back home loves dried mango.

Honest opinion

Hyped, but I would totally skip

Sky Bar at Lebua

The one from The Hangover. The view is not that amazing, it's pretty dead, the vibe isn't great and the drinks are just outrageously expensive.
